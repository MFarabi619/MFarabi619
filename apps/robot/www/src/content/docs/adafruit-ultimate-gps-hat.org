#+title: Ultimate GPS HAT
#+description: Adafruit Ultimate GPS HAT for the Raspberry Pi.

[[https://www.adafruit.com/product/2324][Adafruit Ultimate GPS HAT]] — a
GPS receiver that stacks on the Raspberry Pi's 40-pin header, with a battery
backup for warm starts.

#+ATTR_HTML: :alt Adafruit Ultimate GPS HAT
[[/adafruit-ultimate-gps-hat.jpg]]

* Specifications

Built around the MediaTek PA1616D (MT3333) module.

| Parameter         | Value            |
|-------------------+------------------|
| Satellite systems | GPS + GLONASS    |
| Channels          | 66 tracking / 99 search |
| Update rate       | up to 10 Hz      |
| Sensitivity       | −165 dBm         |
| Backup battery    | CR1220 coin cell |

* Interface

The HAT talks to the Pi over the hardware UART on the 40-pin header — nothing to
wire.

| Signal | Pin        | Notes                              |
|--------+------------+------------------------------------|
| TX/RX  | UART0      | NMEA sentences, 9600 baud default  |
| PPS    | GPIO4      | One pulse-per-second timing output |
| ANT    | u.FL       | Optional external active antenna   |

The HAT takes over the Pi's hardware UART, so the serial console must be freed
first (it can't share the port).

* Onboard features

- *FIX LED* — blinks while searching, ~once every 15 s once it has a fix.
- *CR1220 backup* — keeps the RTC and last almanac alive for warm starts; the
  cell is sold separately.
- *Prototyping area* — a small pad field for soldering extra parts.
- *Patch antenna* on board, with the u.FL connector for an external one.

* Mechanical

65 × 56 × 7 mm, HAT footprint.
